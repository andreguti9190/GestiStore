# Homepage — Persuade

## Audience and job

Shoppers looking for computer components, electronics, or accessories. Help them locate a relevant category or product quickly, compare visible price and stock, and move into the existing purchase flow.

## Confirmed direction

**Índice de repuestos**, selected by the owner on 2026-11-10. Show a direct product search, a clear category index, real product imagery, and aligned price and availability details. The owner asked to retain the AmpereStore logo, blue identity, and current functions.

## First-screen sequence

1. Clear headline and a short description of the existing search, category, price, and availability capabilities.
2. Search field wired to the existing catalog query.
3. Direct catalog route and the existing administrator-only add-product route.
4. One recent product with its real image, category, price in ARS, and stock state.

## Surface constraints

- Preserve product data, role checks, routes, and shopping behavior.
- Avoid unverified delivery, price-leadership, compatibility, or performance promises.
- Use existing product imagery and show a useful empty state when there are no active products.
- Keep the mobile category index and product listing readable without horizontal overflow.

## Build path

Code-led; no image-generation tool is available in this session.

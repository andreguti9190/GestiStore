## Platform

web

## Users

- Shoppers browsing and buying computer components, electronics, and accessories.
- Store operators managing products and sales through the admin-facing interface.

## Product Purpose

AmpereStore is a Spanish-language online store for technology products. Customers browse products, review availability and prices, add items to a cart, complete purchases, and view their orders. Store operators manage the catalog and sales. The owner describes the intended position as a fast, efficient sales system; no performance figures are established.

## Positioning

The intended differentiator is a fast and efficient sales system, as stated by the owner. Do not turn this into a measurable speed or service claim without evidence.

## Operating Context

The login-gated shopper flow includes a homepage, searchable and filterable catalog, product detail pages, cart, checkout, purchase history, and account flows. Store operators have product management and sales views. The product categories include computing, PC components, gaming peripherals, networking, mobile accessories, cables, electronics development, security/domotics, and card readers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing implementation uses Django templates and serves web pages.
- The catalog supports product-name or description search and category filtering.
- Product availability and prices are shown in the catalog and product cards.
- The homepage features the newest active product and displays up to eight more recent active products, plus category links.
- Keep product data, routes, account roles, and existing shopping actions functional during visual work.
- No verified delivery-time, price-leadership, compatibility, customer-review, or performance metrics are recorded.
